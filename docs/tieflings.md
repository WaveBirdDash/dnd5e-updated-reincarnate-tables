[^👺]: Source: _Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes_

[^😈]: The Tiefling was updated in _SRD 5.2_ to provide a Fiendish Legacies option. The Infernal Tiefling is mechanically equivalent to a Tiefling in _SRD 5.1_. The other ancestries are not mechanically equivalent to any prior Tiefling options.
[^👿]: These classifications of Tiefling variants were added in _Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes_ to describe Tieflings with special links to one of the Lords of the Nine Hells, and they have thus been grouped together as variant Infernal Tieflings. The Asmodeus Tiefling is mechanically equivalent to an Infernal Tiefling in _SRD 5.2_. The other ancestries are not mechanically equivalent to any other Tiefling options.
